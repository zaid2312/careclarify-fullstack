Know Your Rights

Under the Clinical Establishments Act and the National Charter of Patients' Rights, you hold enforceable rights in any Indian healthcare facility.

Hospitals cannot charge package rates without providing fully itemized invoice sheets showing medical devices, consumables, and drugs consumed. You have the right to request this ledger before clear-signing discharge releases.

The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) enforces strict pricing ceilings on coronary stents, cardiac pacemakers, orthopedic knee implants, and cancer medications. Charging above these caps constitutes an illegal, reportable offense.

During admission or emergency check-in, hospitals cannot force auto-upgrades to deluxe rooms and charge subsequent proportional doctor fee multipliers without obtaining an explicit written consent signature from family representatives.

Under IRDAI regulations, insurance claims cannot be delayed due to medical record withholding. You are legally entitled to receive copies of discharge sheets, clinical logs, lab results, and surgeon reports within 24 hours of requesting them.
